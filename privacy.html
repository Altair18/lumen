Privacy

Gloam keeps the nest on this device. Your moth’s name, goals, journal and mood stay in the phone or browser. There is no Gloam account.

If you buy Lantern+ on Google Play, Google handles the payment. Gloam does not see your card.

Erase this nest deletes local data. That does not cancel a Play subscription. Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Gloam is for people 13 and older.
